How to use

Import the file into a separate calendar to keep your predicted service dates handy, with a 1-day reminder. Overdue items appear today. Your calendar app controls notifications.

This is a snapshot, not a live sync. After logging a service or updating readings, replace the old imported calendar with a fresh download. Always follow your owner's manual and act on warnings immediately.
